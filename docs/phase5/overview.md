## Why Portfolio Matters More Than You Think

Recruiters spend **6 seconds glancing at your resume**.

But if you have a live demo? They spend **6 minutes**.

A GitHub project with 500 stars? They reconsider your seniority.

The harsh truth: **a great portfolio can get you hired even without a degree. A bad portfolio can't get you hired even with one.**

Why?

- Resume is **claims** ("I built RAG systems")
- GitHub is **proof** (they can see, run, test your code)
- Live demo is **credibility** (the system actually works)

Recruiters trust proof more than claims.

## The 3 Things That Get AI Engineers Interviews

1. **GitHub Portfolio** — code they can evaluate
   - 3-8 polished projects
   - Complete documentation
   - Working demos
   - Clean commit history

2. **Live Demo** — they see it working
   - 2-minute Loom video
   - Short, punchy (problem → solution → result)
   - Shows the value, not slides

3. **Clear Narrative** — why you built what you built
   - "I noticed problem X"
   - "I built Y to solve it"
   - "Achieved result Z"
   - Show intentionality, not random projects

Most engineers have nothing. You'll have all 3.

## Brutal Honesty: What Actually Works

✅ **Works:**
- Personal projects with real impact (hard to fake)
- Loom demos (AI recruiters actually watch them)
- GitHub stars/contributions (signal of quality)
- Specific metrics ("improved latency by 40%")
- Referrals (best way to get interviews)
- Being in the AI community (Twitter, Discord, conferences)

❌ **Doesn't Work:**
- "I'm passionate about AI" (says nothing)
- Fake projects (they'll ask you questions in interview)
- Inflating job titles (they'll check references)
- Resume buzzwords with no depth (HiringManager asks: "Explain RAG")
- Applying blind (worse odds than referral)
- Applying to every job without customization (signals you're not serious)

## Market Reality (2025)

AI engineer demand is **insane**:
- Salary: $150K - $300K+ (depending on level, location)
- Job openings: 100x more than qualified candidates
- Remote options: highly flexible
- Signing bonuses: common ($50K-$150K)

But:
- Companies are picky (want proven track record)
- Interview bars are high (system design, coding, behavioral)
- Equity matters (some companies are better positioned)
- Team quality varies wildly

Your goal: **Find the right company with good team, negotiate hard, learn fast.**
